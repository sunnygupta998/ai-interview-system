Features

Everything You Need to Hire Smarter

Our AI-driven platform automates every step of the technical interview process — from resume parsing to final scoring.

AI Resume Parser

Upload a PDF resume and our AI instantly extracts technical competencies, domain experience, years of expertise, and recommended assessment topics using Groq Llama 3.3 70B.

PDF Scanning Skill Extraction Experience Mapping

Custom MCQ Engine

Dynamically generates tailored multiple-choice questions aligned with each candidate's extracted skills. Configurable difficulty mix: Easy, Medium, and Hard.

Auto-Grading & Breakdown

Instant score evaluation with pass/fail status and detailed topic-by-topic breakdowns. Visualized with clean charts for at-a-glance insights.

Live AI Interviews

Real-time voice-based interviews conducted by AI. The system asks follow-up questions, evaluates responses, and generates comprehensive interview reports.

Admin Controls

Full recruiter dashboard to adjust question counts, test durations, passing thresholds, and difficulty distributions. View all candidate results in one place.

Anti-Cheating Proctoring

Tab-switch detection, webcam monitoring, and copy-paste prevention ensure integrity during assessments. Suspicious activity is auto-flagged for admin review.

How It Works

From Resume to Results in 4 Simple Steps

Our streamlined process takes candidates from PDF upload to fully graded assessment in minutes, not days.

01

Upload Resume

Candidates upload their resume in PDF format. The system accepts any standard resume layout.

02

AI Skill Extraction

Our Groq Llama 3.3 70B model scans the resume, identifying technical skills, domain expertise, and experience levels.

03

Take Assessment

Custom MCQs and live AI interviews are generated based on extracted skills. Candidates complete them in a proctored environment.

04

Instant Results

Scores, pass/fail status, and topic breakdowns are generated immediately. Admins see full reports on their dashboard.
